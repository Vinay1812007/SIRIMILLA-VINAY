About me

Keeping systems connected, building what's next.

I'm Sirimilla Vinay (also written Vinay Sirimilla), a Network Administrator based in Hyderabad, Telangana. Since September 2024 I've worked full-time on-site, setting up and looking after the networks that teams at Jobways.com and, from July 2025, SportsMart.com depend on every day.

I trained as a Computer Operator & Programming Assistant (COPA) at Govt. ITI Mahabubabad and hold a National Trade Certificate. Alongside networking I work as a Product Listing Manager, keeping e-commerce catalogs accurate and ready to sell.

Outside work I build VinaX: an AI coding assistant on npm, a free music streaming app, and an AI assistant inside it. I share what I learn on my YouTube channel, ALL IN ONE WITH VINAY.
