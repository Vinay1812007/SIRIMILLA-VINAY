About me

Networks by day, VinaX by night.

I'm Sirimilla Vinay (you'll also see Vinay Sirimilla), based in Hyderabad, Telangana. Right now I hold three roles: Network Administrator at SportsMart.com since July 2025, Network Administrator at Jobways.com since September 2024, and Product Listing Manager at SportsMart.com, also since July 2025. Both network roles are full-time and on-site.

On the network side I build and look after the LAN, Wi-Fi, routers and switches that teams rely on all day, and I'm the first call when something breaks. As Product Listing Manager at SportsMart.com I make sure every e-commerce listing is accurate, consistent and ready to sell.

My grounding is a National Trade Certificate in Computer Operator & Programming Assistant (COPA) from Govt. ITI Mahabubabad. Outside work I build VinaX, an AI coding assistant on npm, a free music streaming app and the AI assistant inside it, and I share what I learn on my YouTube channel, ALL IN ONE WITH VINAY.
